# Roadmap

What comes next for `wa`, in order.

1. **Linking.** `wa <profile> session start` links your account by QR code or by a pairing code.
2. **Reading.** `wa <profile> serve` receives your history and new messages into the local store; every read and
   search works from it.
3. **Sending** text, then media, replies, reactions and edits.
4. **Groups**, contacts and older history from your phone.
5. **Installers** for Linux, macOS and Windows.
